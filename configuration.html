📌 What is Esusu Fraction?

When a user contributes to a thrift group that has "Apply Esusu deduction" enabled, this percentage is deducted from the contribution amount and credited to the system's Esusu Account.

Example: If fraction = 1% (0.01), and contribution = ₦10,000 → ₦100 goes to Esusu fund, ₦9,900 goes to the group pot.
